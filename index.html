Mobile app for photographers and their clients

An app for photographers who want to manage every photoshoot from first plan to final shot, in collaboration with the client, not just around them.

App runthrough

The problem

Photographers managing a shoot juggle five different tools: texts with the client, notes for outfit ideas, a spreadsheet for gear, a calendar, and email for delivery. Nothing lives in one place, and the client is mostly left out until the day itself.

The approach

Oh Shoot brings it all into one shared space: booking, inspiration, packing and outfit planning, a day-of schedule, and delivery. The photographer plans everything with the client, not around them.

The app works both for the photographer and the client

The concept

Fig-Monkey is there to gently remind you to reconnect with your own needs while you work, and to lighten up an atmosphere that can otherwise get pretty stressful.

Documentation & Customization

Designing a public asset meant making the tool accessible and intuitive for others. I wrote clear onboarding instructions covering setup and timer customization, while building out flexible color options so users could easily adapt the monkey to their personal taste and screens.

Digital store

A digital store focused on tactile product presentation, glass and frosted-bottle effects, and a smooth shopping flow across desktop and mobile.

The details

The starting point for this project was an existing brand: Spicehaus. The goal was to find where micro-animations could add character and personality to the shopping experience, without tipping into anything overdone or distracting. Simplicity and ease of use stayed the priority throughout, with the animation work layered on top rather than driving the design.

The Final Dinner

at Black's Manor
RSVP landing page & event app

The concept borrows the format of a murder mystery party, with the interaction style inspired by the board game Cluedo.

Kids Against The Machine

by Noga Erez
Vinyl design

Handmade vinyl packaging built around Noga Erez's fully acoustic album, Kids Against the Machine.

The concept

This is the acoustic version of Kids. I went with handwritten type and an anarchistic, raw vibe, since the album is about rebelling against machines, technology, and effects. Everything on it was recorded practically, so the design follows that same spirit.
